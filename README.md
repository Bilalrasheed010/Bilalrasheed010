## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🎓 [EduPrep](https://github.com/Bilalrasheed010/EduPrep): Online Examination & Practice Platform
ASP.NET MVC web application for exams and student practice, with role-based access for admins, teachers and students.
- Question bank organised by level, subject and topic, with bulk question import
- Timed tests, attempts and results, plus leaderboards
- Subscription plans with payment verification, notices and certificates
- 🔗 [Live demo](https://eduprep.somee.com) · [Source code](https://github.com/Bilalrasheed010/EduPrep)

`ASP.NET MVC` `C#` `Entity Framework 6` `SQL Server` `BCrypt`

### 💊 [PharmacyERP](https://github.com/Bilalrasheed010/PharmacyERP): Pharmacy Distribution ERP
ASP.NET MVC 5 ERP for a pharmacy distributor, with separate **Admin** and **Medical Store** portals.
- Products, batches and expiry tracking, with stock adjustments
- Purchases, sales and returns, receipts and payments
- Customer and supplier ledgers, trial balance and audit trail
- Reports for sales, purchases, profit, stock valuation and outstanding balances
- 🔗 [Source code](https://github.com/Bilalrasheed010/PharmacyERP)

`ASP.NET MVC 5` `C#` `Entity Framework 6` `SQL Server`

### 🌐 [Bilal Portfolio](https://github.com/Bilalrasheed010/Bilal-Portfolio): React + Three.js
Personal portfolio with interactive 3D visuals and scroll animation.
